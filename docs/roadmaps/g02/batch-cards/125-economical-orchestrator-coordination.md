## Objective

Make the normal orchestrator an economical coordinator while moving material
planning to operator-confirmed chatterbox packets and substantive PR review to
independent child reviewers.

## Scope

- revise orchestrator, chatterbox, direct-review, and mechanical-projection
  modes where their authority changes;
- propagate the compact role split through working rules, reusable doctrine,
  copy-ready templates, protocol kernel, architecture, operator guidance, and
  skill outcomes;
- define Paseo review-child launch, parentage, notification, reuse, and
  replacement behavior;
- preserve the existing diversified-routing, lane-local failure, exact-head,
  checks, ancestry, mergeability, and operator-pause gates;
- keep structural validation structural; record semantic oracle rows as
  exact-head review evidence;
- update parity, card, milestone, one closeout log, handoff, and front doors;
- open a reviewable PR and stop.

Out of scope: Paseo profile edits, running the ten-PR trial, product planning,
opening a chatterbox, changing the chatterbox shared-checkout protocol, or
weakening review/merge requirements.
